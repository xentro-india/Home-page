What is Xentro ?

A connected startup ecosystem built around relevance.

Xentro is the startup ecosystem platform built to bring entrepreneurs and the people who support them closer together.

We connect startups with opportunities and resources through one connected ecosystem. Instead of navigating different platforms and networks, users can discover what is relevant to them in one place.

From finding the right mentor to discovering an opportunity or connecting with an investor, Xentro helps turn discovery into meaningful connections.
